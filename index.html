About The Festival

A Global Platform for Short Film Excellence

The Global Film Festival 2025 is an international short film competition hosted in the vibrant city of Dubai. We invite filmmakers from around the world to showcase their creativity, vision, and storytelling prowess through short films that captivate, inspire, and challenge audiences.

Eligibility

  • Films must be under 30 minutes in length (including credits)
  • All genres are welcome: fiction, documentary, experimental
  • Films must have been completed after January 1, 2023
  • Submissions must include English subtitles if the original language is not English

Our Mission

To discover and promote exceptional filmmaking talent from across the globe, providing a prestigious platform for emerging filmmakers to gain international recognition and launch their careers to new heights.
